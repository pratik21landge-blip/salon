THE HUSTLE STORY

From P&L Spreadsheets to Precision Fades

Just like Pune's famous MBA Bhurji revolutionized street food by bringing standardization, hygiene, and corporate pride to the humble egg cart—we did the same for grooming.

After graduating from B-school and grinding in corporate finance for 6 years, our founder Nikhil realized men and women in tech corridors were sick of either paying ₹2,500 at pretentious 5-star hotel salons or dealing with unhygienic local shops with stale towels.

MBA Hair Salon was born with one simple thesis: Five-star hygiene, master-level craftsmanship, zero waiting, and honest street-smart prices.

"A haircut shouldn't feel like a corporate negotiation. You sit in the chair, sip great chai, look sharp, and get back to your hustle."

Nikhil Sharma MBA (Finance), Founder & CEO
